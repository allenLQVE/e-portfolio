title: Daily Email Notifier

Everyday in the morning, the first thing I do is to open my phone and check the social medias. This was my daily routine and wasted lost of my time. This email notifier allows users to receive news and updates on the services that they are interested in, such as social media, weather informations, news, or blogs.

This project used Java with several different design patterns as this project was built for the course that studies design patterns. In this project, I have intergreated API from [openWeather](https://openweathermap.org/) and [newsapi](https://newsapi.org/). By utilizing the decorator and builder pattern, new API that provide different news and update can be easily intergrated with the application.
